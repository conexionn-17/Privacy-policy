# Sylvie — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 9, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Sylvie is an AI-powered personal assistant designed to help users manage LinkedIn-related workflows, generate content suggestions, and review proposed actions.

This Privacy Policy explains how Sylvie handles information when users interact with the application.

## 2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features enabled, Sylvie may process:

* **LinkedIn information:** Basic profile information and authorized post or engagement data accessible through LinkedIn's approved APIs.
* **User instructions:** Commands, preferences, and feedback submitted through Telegram or other supported messaging platforms.
* **AI-generated content:** Draft posts, suggested comments, and summaries created in response to user requests.
* **Technical information:** Application logs, timestamps, and error information required to operate and troubleshoot the service.

Sylvie will only request and process information necessary for its enabled features and granted permissions.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

* Generate personalized post and comment suggestions.
* Process user commands and approval decisions.
* Provide authorized LinkedIn functionality.
* Prevent duplicate actions and maintain an activity history.
* Maintain application security and troubleshoot errors.

Sylvie will not intentionally publish content or perform an engagement action without the required user approval, where approval is part of the configured workflow.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Sylvie may rely on third-party services, including:

* **LinkedIn:** For account authentication and authorized LinkedIn functionality.
* **Groq:** For AI-powered text generation and analysis when enabled.
* **Telegram:** For user commands, notifications, and approval interactions when enabled.
* **Cloud hosting and database providers:** To run the application and store necessary operational data.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Sylvie aims to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect stored information, including access controls and secure handling of API credentials.

Access tokens, passwords, and API secrets should not be exposed in public repositories, messages, or application logs.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Sharing

Sylvie does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers necessary to operate its features or disclosed when required by applicable law.

The application should only share information with third parties as necessary for the requested functionality.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain required records, or meet applicable legal obligations.

Users may request deletion of their stored information by contacting the application operator through the contact method provided with the application.

Where applicable, users may also revoke Sylvie's access through LinkedIn's account settings.

## 8. User Choices and Permissions

Users control the permissions they grant to Sylvie and may revoke authorized access where supported by the relevant platform.

Users should review suggested content before approving publication or engagement actions.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Sylvie is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Sylvie's features, integrations, and data practices change. The latest version will be published at the application's privacy policy URL.

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the application operator using the contact information published with Sylvie.
